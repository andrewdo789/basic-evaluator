# Research judgment, self-test 01 (level-7 placement test)

**Clock:** about 45 minutes in total, 2 minutes per question. **No AI, no notes, no searching.** Write each answer below
its question in two to five sentences: the point, plus one concrete example or paper where the question asks for one.
**Pass:** 16 of 22, each graded pass or fail against a key. The questions cover the core of current AI safety research:
alignment, oversight, control, evaluations, interpretability, and chain-of-thought monitoring.

1. What is the difference between outer and inner alignment? Give one example of each.
2. Why might RLHF produce sycophancy? What evidence supports that?
3. Distinguish reward hacking, specification gaming, and goal misgeneralization.
4. What did Sleeper Agents show, and what does it not show about natural deception?
5. What exactly did the alignment faking paper observe, and what are its main caveats?
6. What is a model organism? What makes one good or bad for testing a detection method?
7. What is the difference between control and alignment? Why might a lab want both?
8. Walk through trusted monitoring. What sets the audit budget, and what does the red team optimize?
9. How would you make a monitor benchmark like SLEIGHT-Bench harder to overfit?
10. What is superposition, and why does it make neuron-level interpretability hard?
11. What does an SAE optimize? Name two known failure modes of SAE features.
12. What is an attribution graph? What does the replacement model leave out, and why does that matter?
13. How does activation patching differ from ablation? When would each mislead you?
14. You trained a linear probe with 95% accuracy. What baselines and controls do you need before believing it?
15. Is chain-of-thought faithful? Is it monitorable? Why are those different questions?
16. Why is training against a CoT monitor risky?
17. What is an activation oracle, and how would you check it is not just reading the prompt?
18. How would you tell whether a capability eval under-elicited the model?
19. What is an RSP? What happens at Anthropic when a model crosses an ASL threshold?
20. Why did GCG attacks transfer to closed models? What makes a jailbreak defense credible?
21. Pick a recent AI safety paper you know: what is the one experiment you would run next, and what result would change your mind?
22. Which open problem in AI safety would you work on, and why you?
